## Methodology Notes
Counts reflect criminal victimizations, not unique individuals. A person involved in more than one incident will appear in the data more than once, consistent with FBI/NIBRS reporting standards.
A single victim may be associated with multiple offense types within the same incident (e.g. Simple Assault and Intimidation charged together). Offense-level counts will not sum to the total victim count — this is expected.
Victim and offender counts will not match. One incident can have multiple victims, multiple offenders, or both.

## Additional Info
Data source: FBI National Incident-Based Reporting System (NIBRS), Wisconsin 2024. Domestic violence incidents are defined using the FBI's criteria: a qualifying offense must have occurred AND the offender must have had a recognized domestic or intimate relationship with the victim.
